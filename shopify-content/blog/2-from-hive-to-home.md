## Content (paste below this line)

Every jar of BeePops Himalayan Honey starts its journey far from the city, in the flowering valleys of the Himalayas, where bees forage on wild blossoms at altitude, away from heavy farming and pollution.

### 1. The hive

Our honey comes from beekeepers who care for their colonies the traditional way. Bees are never overfed with sugar syrup, and hives are only harvested when the honey is ready, so the colony always keeps enough for itself.

### 2. The harvest

Once the comb is capped, the honey is gently extracted. No high heat, no chemical treatment. Heating honey makes it look clearer on the shelf, but it destroys the natural enzymes, pollen, and aroma that make raw honey special. We skip that step on purpose.

### 3. Filtering, not processing

We strain the honey just enough to remove wax and bits of comb. That's it. Because it's raw, you may notice it crystallise in cooler weather. That's a sign of real, unprocessed honey, not a fault. Just place the jar in warm water and it will turn liquid again.

### 4. Tested and packed

Every batch is checked for purity before it's packed, so what reaches you is exactly what the bees made: nothing added, nothing taken away.

### 5. Your home

From a spoon of honey in warm water in the morning to a drizzle on your oats or roti, our Himalayan Honey is made to be part of your everyday routine.

From hive to home, we keep it simple, honest, and pure. Just the way nature intended.
