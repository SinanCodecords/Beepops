## Content (paste below this line)

In India, protein is often misunderstood, with "healthy bars" wrapped in claims but lacking real nourishment. That's where we decided to change the game.

Our BeePops Mini Protein Bar is crafted to deliver maximum nutrition in every bite, without compromising on ingredients or values:

- Made with wholesome millets, peanuts, jaggery, wheat, and honey
- Provides 15g protein per 100g
- Free from synthetic flavouring, colouring, or preservatives

### Why millets?

Millets have been part of Indian kitchens for centuries. They are naturally rich in fibre, keep you full for longer, and release energy slowly, so you don't get the sugar spike and crash that comes with most packaged snacks. Paired with peanuts for protein and honey and jaggery for natural sweetness, they make a snack your body actually knows what to do with.

### Small bar, big reach

We've already served this bar to 10,000+ Anganwadi children through our partnerships, proving that high-quality protein can be real, simple, and accessible.

### Made for everyday life

Whether you're a student, an office-goer, or a parent looking for smart lunchbox choices, our mini bar brings nutrition, taste, and trust together in one small yet powerful bite. Keep one in your bag, your desk drawer, or your child's tiffin.

Because clean nutrition isn't a trend. It's a right. And we're making sure it reaches every hand.
