# Privacy policy

**Where:** Shopify Admin → Settings → Policies → Privacy policy

> Shopify can also generate a full legal privacy policy for you: click "Create from template" (or turn on "Automated privacy policy"). That version covers Shopify's own legal details. Use it, or use this simpler one, or paste this at the top of Shopify's version.

---

## Content (paste below this line)

BeePops ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit beepops.in or buy from us, and how we use it.

### What we collect

- **Order details:** your name, email, phone number, and shipping and billing address.
- **Payment details:** payments are processed securely by Shopify and our payment partners. We never see or store your full card or UPI details.
- **Account details:** if you create an account, your login email and your order history.
- **Browsing information:** basic device, browser, and usage data collected through cookies, to keep the site working and improve it.
- **Messages:** anything you send us through forms, email, or the newsletter signup.

### How we use it

- To process, ship, and deliver your orders
- To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and replies to your questions
- To send offers and news, only if you subscribed (you can unsubscribe any time)
- To prevent fraud and keep the site secure
- To understand how people use our site so we can improve it

### Who we share it with

We only share what's needed with the services that help us run the store:

- Shopify, which hosts our store and checkout
- Payment providers, to take payment
- Courier partners, to deliver your order
- Email and analytics tools, to communicate with you and understand site traffic

We never sell your personal information.

### Cookies

We use cookies to keep your cart and login working and to understand site traffic. You can turn cookies off in your browser, but parts of the site, like the cart, may stop working.

### How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as required for tax and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time, unless we're required by law to keep it.

### Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@beepops.in.

### Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
