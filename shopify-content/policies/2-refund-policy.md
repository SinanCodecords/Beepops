## Content (paste below this line)

Because our products are food, we can't accept returns of opened or used items for hygiene and safety reasons. But if something isn't right with your order, we'll make it right.

### When we replace or refund

We offer a free replacement or a full refund if:

- Your product arrived damaged, leaking, or with a broken seal
- You received the wrong product or quantity
- The product was past its best-before date on delivery

### How to request it

1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@beepops.in or +91 95571 06284.
2. Include your order number and clear photos of the product and the packaging.
3. Our team will review it and reply within [2] business days.

You may be asked to keep the product until we confirm. In most cases you won't need to send anything back.

### What we can't refund

- Opened or partly used products, unless the product itself is faulty
- Requests made more than 48 hours after delivery
- Changes in taste preference
- Orders that couldn't be delivered because of a wrong address or because nobody was available to receive them

### Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by contacting us. Once an order has shipped, it can't be cancelled.

### How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within [5–7] business days. For cash on delivery orders, we'll refund to your bank account or UPI ID.
